The Problem with Online Legacy JWT Decoders

  • Privacy Policy Violations: Unencrypted claim verification on remote nodes violates modern privacy regulations.
  • Network Dependency: Legacy tools fail when working within air-gapped corporate environments or isolated staging VPCs.
