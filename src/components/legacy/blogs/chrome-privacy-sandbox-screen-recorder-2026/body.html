The Problem with Traditional Cloud Screen Recorders

For years, users turned to desktop software installers or commercial Chrome extensions for quick screen capture. However, traditional SaaS solutions present severe drawbacks in today's privacy-focused environment:

  • Privacy Risk: Video frames and microphone audio are transmitted to remote servers, exposing enterprise secrets or personally identifiable information (PII) to server breaches or unauthorized AI model training.
  • Performance Cost: Heavy desktop software and background extension processes consume significant CPU and RAM, causing frame drops during resource-intensive tasks like gaming or 3D rendering.
  • Compatibility & Paywalls: Many cloud services enforce arbitrary recording time limits (e.g., 5 minutes max), slap unwanted watermarks on exported files, or require account creation and paid subscriptions just to download your own recording.
