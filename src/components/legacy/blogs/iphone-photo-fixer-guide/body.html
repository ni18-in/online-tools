HEIC vs. JPG vs. PNG: Which Format Should You Pick?

Choosing the right output format preserves image quality while keeping file sizes manageable for sharing, archival, or web publishing:

  • JPG (Recommended for Photos): Delivers compact file sizes and universal support across Windows File Explorer, email clients, CMS uploads, and government or job application portals.
  • PNG (Lossless Quality): Best when you need zero compression artifacts for screenshots, diagrams, receipts, or graphics you intend to edit further.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my iPhone photos uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion happens strictly inside your browser using client-side WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas APIs, so personal and family photos stay completely private on your device.

Does batch conversion preserve image resolution and EXIF orientation?

Yes. Portrait and landscape orientation tags are decoded automatically before encoding to JPG or PNG, preventing sideways photos on Windows PCs.
