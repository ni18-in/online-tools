Step-by-Step: How to Record Your Screen Free

  1. Navigate to the Screen Recorder Pro Tool page.
  2. Select your preferred audio settings (enable microphone audio or system audio if required).
  3. Click the Start Recording button to launch the browser permission prompt.
  4. Choose what you want to share: Entire Screen, Window, or Chrome/Browser Tab. Be sure to check "Share audio" if capturing tab audio.
  5. When you are finished recording, click Stop Recording or use the floating screen share banner stop button.
  6. Preview your video in the built-in player and click Download Video to save the file locally to your device.

Best Practices for High-Quality Screen Recording

  • Close Unnecessary Apps: Free up system memory and prevent unwanted desktop popups during your recording session.
  • Test Microphone Audio: Perform a short 5-second test recording to verify your audio levels and background noise before starting a long presentation.
  • Use Tab Recording for Demos: When presenting web applications, recording a single tab provides the cleanest visual result without desktop clutter.
